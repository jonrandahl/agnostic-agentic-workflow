# Job Application Strategy: Framing Redundancy

## 1. Phrasing "Reason for Leaving" on Application Forms

### General Professional Terms

* Role affected by restructuring
* Position eliminated due to business reorganization
* Contract ended due to company downsizing
* Team transitioned following budget adjustments

### Specific to Current Situation (Financial Difficulties & Team Reorganization)

* **Role eliminated due to team reorganisation**
* **Position redundant following department restructure**
* **Role affected by company financial restructuring**
* **Position closed due to operational reorganisation**

### Specific to Past Situation (Role Consolidated/Streamlined)

* **Role eliminated due to departmental restructure**
* **Position redundant following team reorganisation**
* **Role affected by company restructuring**

---

## 2. Interview Verbal Scripts

### Combined Explanation for Two Redundancies
>
> "The first instance was a structural reorganisation where my two-person department
> was streamlined, and the company chose to consolidate the responsibilities into a
> single role. In the second, more recent instance, the business faced broader
> financial difficulties, which required a complete team restructure and budget cuts.
> In both cases, the decisions were purely corporate and down to shifting business
> models, which has simply made me more adaptable and eager to find a stable team
> where I can add long-term value."

### Isolated Explanation for the Recent Role
>
> "My previous company faced some financial difficulties, which led to a budget-driven reorganisation of the team. As part of that structural change, my specific role was eliminated and the remaining management responsibilities were redistributed. While it was a shame to leave, it was strictly a business decision, and I am incredibly proud of what I achieved there. I am now looking forward to bringing those skills to a new challenge."

---

## 3. Handling Follow-Up Questions

### Q1: "Why did they choose your role specifically?"

* **Strategy**: Focus on business structure, not performance.
* **Response**: "The company needed to flatten the management structure to cut costs. The duties of my role were absorbed by other departments, making the position itself redundant under the new budget."

### Q2: "How did the reorganisation affect the team?"

* **Strategy**: Keep the answer objective and focus on the operational shift.
* **Response**: "The team was restructured to report directly to a different division to streamline operations. It was a purely financial decision to keep the department running with lower overheads."

### Q3: "Did anyone else leave, or just you?"

* **Strategy**: Shift the focus from numbers to structural changes. Avoid personal opinions or internal politics.
* **Response**: "The restructuring targeted specific management roles to meet a budget goal, and mine was the one affected in our department. It allowed the company to reorganise the team's reporting lines."

---

## 4. Key Rules for Success

1. **Stick to the Official Reason**: Always align your written and verbal answers with the official company record for references.
2. **Remove Emotion**: Keep personal opinions regarding internal politics or colleagues completely out of the conversation.
3. **Pivot to the Future**: Always conclude a verbal explanation by turning the focus back to why you are excited about the new role you are applying for.
